# Sources — the data-architecture / Data Mesh canon

The external best-practice this skill cites. **Cite-and-link**; never reproduce reserved text. Verified against the primary source at research time (2026-06). These are the **generic floor**; the always-first `data-mesh-profile.md` encodes the cross-org/no-trusted-operator use-case that **overrides** some of this for the federated setting. ## Principles & canon

- **§principles — Dehghani's four principles.** (1) domain-oriented decentralized data ownership & architecture; (2) **data as a product**; (3) self-serve data infrastructure as a platform; (4) **federated computational governance**. The 2019 article introduced the three convergences (DDD + product thinking + self-serve platform); the **2020** article added the fourth principle + the canonical four-principle framing; the 2022 O'Reilly book *Data Mesh: Delivering Data-Driven Value at Scale* canonized them. — https://martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html (2020) + https://martinfowler.com/articles/data-monolith-to-mesh.html (2019). Vendor framings (AWS, Databricks) keep the four but drop "computational" and bend the platform principle toward their product; `datamesh-architecture.com` is the most canon-faithful community reference. — https://aws.amazon.com/what-is/data-mesh/ · https://www.databricks.com/blog/what-is-data-mesh · https://www.datamesh-architecture.com/

- **§data-product — the architectural quantum + the six attributes.** A data product is "the smallest unit of architecture that can be independently deployed with high functional cohesion" — encapsulating **code** (pipelines/APIs/policy), **data + metadata**, and **infrastructure**. The six usability attributes (verified at the 2019 article): **discoverable, addressable, trustworthy** (SLOs on quality/integrity), **self-describing, interoperable** (global standards), **secure**. *(The acronym "DATSIS" and the formal input/output/control/discovery **port** taxonomy are a community mnemonic / attributed to the 2022 book — cite the six **attributes** as canon, not "DATSIS" as Dehghani's own term; see Verify-before-print.)* — https://martinfowler.com/articles/data-monolith-to-mesh.html

- **§data-contracts — the producer↔consumer interface.** Schema + semantics + SLAs/SLOs + quality rules + versioning. The consolidating standard is the **Open Data Contract Standard (ODCS)**, **v3.1.0 (Dec 2025)**, governed by **Bitol** (incubation under LF AI & Data, Apache-2.0); sections = fundamentals/schema/quality/SLA/servers/team/support. The **Data Contract CLI** (`datacontract-cli`) validates against ODCS and tests real data. **The older standalone "Data Contract Specification" is deprecated** in favor of ODCS (support through end-2026) — target ODCS for new work. — https://bitol.io/ + https://github.com/bitol-io/open-data-contract-standard · https://docs.datacontract.com/

- **§federated-governance — global vs local, enforced computationally.** Domain models stay local/sovereign; cross-domain concerns (identity/entities, addressing, security, the ontology) are **globally standardized** by a **guild/federation** and **automatically enforced by the platform** ("computational") rather than by manual committee review. *("Policy-as-code" is the industry implementation label, not a Dehghani term.)* — https://martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html · https://www.datamesh-architecture.com/

- **§self-serve-platform — the three planes.** (1) data-infrastructure provisioning plane; (2) **data-product developer-experience plane** (the day-to-day plane — high-level abstractions); (3) data-mesh supervision plane (discovery, cross-product correlation, mesh-level governance). Purpose: reduce domain-team **cognitive load**; the platform is domain-agnostic, domains own products + semantics + SLOs. — https://martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html

- **§observability — data quality, observability, lineage.** The **five pillars of data observability** (Monte Carlo's framing, now de-facto common vocabulary — attribute as such, not as Dehghani canon): **freshness, volume, distribution, schema, lineage** — they operationalize the "trustworthy" attribute as SLOs. **OpenLineage** is the open lineage-metadata standard (LF AI & Data **Graduate** project, graduated Jul 2023): dataset/job/run entities, OpenAPI spec, extensible via **facets**. — https://www.montecarlodata.com/blog-introducing-the-5-pillars-of-data-observability/ · https://openlineage.io/ + https://lfaidata.foundation/projects/openlineage/

- **§interoperability — cross-domain/cross-platform exchange.** Global standardization of cross-cutting concerns (identity, addressing conventions, polyglot output formats). The product-descriptor layer (above contract-level ODCS): **DPDS** (Data Product Descriptor Specification, opendatamesh) and **ODPS** (Open Data Product Specification, Linux Foundation — expanding into a "standards family" in 2026). — https://dpds.opendatamesh.org/ · https://opendataproducts.org/

- **§fit-and-failure-modes — when mesh is NOT the answer.** Data Mesh is **sociotechnical and org-scale**; prerequisite is org maturity, not tooling. Documented failure modes: **governance theater** (a central team in disguise / domain folders in dbt), **ownership drift** + producer↔consumer misalignment, **federated-governance gridlock**, **data-product sprawl**, and **premature adoption** (small orgs with small data "probably shouldn't" — if you can keep centralized control without sacrificing value, you probably should). Mesh (org/sociotechnical) vs **data fabric** (tech metadata/integration layer) vs **lakehouse** (storage/compute platform) vs warehouse — different stack layers, often **complementary**, not competing. — https://montecarlo.ai/blog-when-data-mesh-governance-doesnt-work · https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/data-lakehouse-vs-data-fabric-vs-data-mesh

## Verify-before-print (do not assert as fact until checked at primary source)

- **"DATSIS" + the input/output/control/discovery port taxonomy:** the six *attributes* are verified canon; the **acronym** and the four-**port** model were not confirmed at a primary source — attributed to the 2022 O'Reilly book. Cite the six attributes; treat DATSIS/ports as a teaching mnemonic, verify against the book before asserting as Dehghani's exact terms.
- **Data Contract Specification deprecation:** time-sensitive — standalone-spec support ends **end of 2026**; new work targets **ODCS**. Re-check before relying on either.
- **Product-descriptor standards (ODPS/DPDS) are unsettled in 2026** (ODPS announced a "standards family" expansion mid-2026) — present this layer as *active/unsettled*; verify the current state at design time rather than treating one spec as the winner. Contract-level *is* consolidating on ODCS.
- **"Five pillars" + "policy-as-code"** are industry/vendor vocabulary (Monte Carlo; general), **not** part of Dehghani's original definition — attribute them as such, don't present them as canon.
- **All version numbers / "current as of 2026" claims** (ODCS v3.1.0, OpenLineage status) are point-in-time — re-verify at authoring time; the data-mesh tooling space moves fast.
